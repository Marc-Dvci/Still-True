What happens when the world moved

  1. Re-checked on time. Every scheduled post is read again 60 and 10 minutes before it goes out, and every few hours before that.
  2. Held, never deleted. High-confidence problems (sold out, cancelled, a date already passed, wording that echoes an active disaster) move the post to drafts in Buffer. Everything else gets a note.
  3. Explained inside Buffer. A note on the post and a tag say what changed and where it was read, so the team sees it in Buffer itself.
  4. One tap to fix or release. A corrected price, the right weekday or the next dry slot is proposed. Release puts the post back at its original time.
