Privacy

Still True is an independent app built on the Buffer API by Marc Donovici. It is not an official Buffer product. It reads your Buffer queue to check scheduled posts against the world, and keeps only what that check needs.

What it reads from Buffer

  • Your Buffer account id, name and timezone, and your organisations.
  • Your channels: name, network, timezone and avatar.
  • Scheduled posts, and drafts that have a due time, for the next seven days: text, due time, creation time, channel, tags and link fields.

Buffer asks you to approve these permissions when you connect: account:read, posts:read, posts:write, notes and offline_access.

What it writes to Buffer

  • When a problem is certain and your setting is "Hold", it moves the post to drafts. It never deletes a post.
  • It adds a note to a flagged post saying what changed and where it was read, and one of two tags: "Still True: held" or "Still True: review".
  • When you press Release, it schedules the post again. When you press Apply fix, it changes the post's text or time as shown.
  • When you press "Add sample drafts", it creates five drafts on the channel you choose. Drafts do not publish.

What it stores, and for how long

  • Your Buffer access and refresh tokens, encrypted with AES-GCM, in a storage space that belongs to your account alone (a Cloudflare Durable Object).
  • Your settings: audience country, business location if you set one, and the hold policy.
  • For each post in the next seven days: its text, links, due times, verdict and findings. A post is removed from storage once it leaves the queue, unless Still True is holding it.
  • The facts read from linked pages (status, price, stock, event date) for a few hours, and a log of the actions Still True took.

Disconnect and delete my data (in Settings) revokes the token at Buffer and erases everything above. Removing the app in Buffer's settings also stops all access.

Who else is contacted

  • The pages linked in your posts, fetched with a "StillTrue" user agent to read their price, stock or event date.
  • Open-Meteo, with your business location only, if you set one, for the forecast.
  • GDACS and Wikipedia, for disaster alerts and the day's news. Nothing about you is sent.
  • Cloudflare Workers AI, only when you press "Suggest wording": the post text and the finding are sent to draft new wording. Checks themselves never use a model.

What it does not do

No advertising, no analytics, no tracking cookies, no sale or sharing of data. The site sets two cookies: a signed session cookie and a ten-minute cookie used only while you sign in with Buffer.
